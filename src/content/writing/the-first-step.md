Hi there! Let's get right to it: we're in a golden age for getting our hands dirty and doing stuff. If you're interested in something, a 7-year-old laptop and an internet connection can get you very far. There are a lot of people (myself included!) who can clear that bar, and I think more of us should be getting our hands dirty with interpretability[^2]. So I'm learning. You should too!

There are lots of theories floating around about what interp can or won't prove, and while I love a good theory, I think that we should err on the side of *doing experiments*. It's easier than ever to get our hands on models and test our ideas, so why not? We might even find that our theories get better when we have a little empirical ground to stand on! Maybe. If we don't fool ourselves...

But fooling ourselves is a topic that others can handle[^3], I'm here to give you a handle on interp. Here: 👋
